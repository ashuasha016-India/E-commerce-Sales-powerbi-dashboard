# 🛍️ E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, customer behavior,
product performance, payment methods, order status, geographic sales and monthly sales trend.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling

## 📌 Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Average Discount

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

- Sales by Month
- Sales by Category
- Top Products by Sales
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales by City
- Sales by Payment Method
- Orders by Status

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest sales among the product categories.
- Regular customers contributed the largest share of sales among customer segments.
- Tehran generated the highest sales among the cities shown in the dashboard.
- Gateway payments accounted for the largest share of sales among payment methods.
- Completed orders represented the largest portion of total orders.
- January recorded the highest monthly sales in the year.
- Headphones generated the highest sales among the electronic products.

## 💡 Business Opportunities

- Investigate lower-performing cities to identify opportunities for targeted marketing.
- Analyze lower-contributing customer segments for targeted promotions and retention.
- Review lower-performing products for pricing and promotional opportunities.
- Use monthly sales patterns to improve promotional and inventory planning.
